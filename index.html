About us

We help businesses communicate with relevance and build trust across Egypt and selected MENA markets.

Established in 2019, MENA Company PR is a full-service public relations, communications and operational consultancy dedicated to amplifying multinational enterprises, emerging brands, executive profiles and organisational initiatives across the Middle East and North Africa.

Combining regional media ecosystems, regulatory know-how and a nuanced understanding of local markets, we serve as a trusted bridge for international clients seeking strategic visibility, institutional relationships and authentic market impact.

Purpose

Help worthwhile businesses be understood and trusted in the markets they enter.

Vision

Become a trusted Egypt-to-MENA communications partner for growing businesses.

Mission

Combine local insight, bilingual narratives and relevant media relationships to deliver communication with a clear purpose and measurable results.

Core capabilities & services

Five pillars.One regional partner.

From the first market study to the last press call of a summit, each pillar works on its own or as part of one joined-up programme.

A

Strategic Advisory & Market Intelligence

Understand the market before you enter it.

  • Regional PR & advertising needs assessment

    In-depth feasibility analyses and strategic gap assessments for international organisations entering the MENA landscape — cultural resonance, competitor positioning and high-yield communication channels.

  • Market feasibility & sectoral studies

    Tailored market research, landscape evaluations and corporate entry roadmaps focused on the Egyptian and Arab markets, to support executive investment and operational decisions.

B

Strategic Media Relations & Thought Leadership

Credible visibility with regional business media.

  • Tier-1 editorial relations & placements

    Senior editorial relationships across regional publications, pursuing positive press exposure, executive interviews and high-impact feature stories.

  • Curated regional media distribution

    Tailored access to directories of daily newspapers, digital publications and news agencies across the Gulf & Yemen, Lebanon & the Levant, and North Africa & the Maghreb.

  • Journalists & research fellows directory

    Strategic matchmaking with specialised Arab journalists, sector correspondents, academic researchers and regional think tanks.

  • Digital placement & SEO backlinking

    Article placements on high-authority regional news portals in English and Arabic, with contextual backlinks that support search visibility and brand equity.

C

Narrative Development & Multilingual Content

Stories written for each audience — in Arabic and English.

  • Brand positioning & strategic storytelling

    Compelling brand, leadership and policy narratives that bridge international visions with regional cultural nuance.

  • Editorial production & content creation

    Journalistic-standard press releases, executive op-eds, white papers and thought-leadership articles that meet strict media guidelines.

  • Culturally-tailored digital engagement

    Social strategies across LinkedIn, X and Instagram that combine brand storytelling with cultural appropriateness and regional nuance.

D

Government Relations, Permitting & Corporate Facilitation

Permits, accreditations and local procedures, handled with care.

  • Official media permitting & accreditations

    Navigating governmental protocols for press credentials, filming permits, institutional clearances and media authorisations for international delegations and production teams.

  • Corporate & industrial facilitation (Egypt)

    Liaison support for corporations and industrial firms finalising commercial contracts, industrial land allocations and local regulatory procedures, through trusted on-the-ground partners.
